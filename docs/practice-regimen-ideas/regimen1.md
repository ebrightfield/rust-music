
### Goal:
- Light, open-ended
- Meant to be doable daily
- A focus on musical gestures, not technique or theory (this is turning out not to be the case, and it's more about technique)
- Preferably, don't look at your hands or the fretboard. Close your eyes.

### Warmup -- Five Minutes
- Permutation of LH Fingers WXYZ on a string
- Up the strings in a position.
- Then shift up positions.
- Constant duration
- Soft, mezzo piano
- Focus is on relaxation, muscle and breath control

### Part One -- Four Minutes
- Take thirty seconds to decide on a musical gesture using <INTERVAL>.
- Metronome steady, slow, random between 54-78.
- Play that gesture in all the positions, in the same key.

### Part Two -- Five Minutes
- Repeat Part One, this time with <3NC>.
Preferably, the chord contains the previous interval.

### Part Three -- Four Minutes
- Repeat Part One, this time with <4NC>.
Preferably, the chord contains the previous 3NC.

### Cooldown: -- Two Minutes
- Permutation of <7NS>.
- One random position / pattern.
- Many keys.
- Steady metronome.

### Global Configuration Parameters
Some of these could be randomly chosen for the day.
- Focused Interval
- Focused String

- Focused meter

- Whitelist (break this down into subcategories, for each 3NC, 4NC, 5NS, 7NS, symmetrical scale, irregular scales, etc. maybe make presets that are "only those in the major scale")

- Build pitch sets up from supersets of previous smaller pitch sets (vs just picking random)
